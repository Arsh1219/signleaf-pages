Scanning and signing

How do I sign a document?

Choose Scan Document on Home, scan the paper pages, and review their order and crop. Open the editor, add a saved signature or scan a new one, and move or resize it. Preview every page, choose PDF, PNG or JPG, then tap Save.

My signature looks faint or has a shadow

Use dark ink on plain paper in even light. Crop close to the signature without removing dots or fine strokes. Adjust Cleanup and Ink Strength while comparing the original. Retake the photo if the result does not preserve your handwriting.

Where are my documents?

Open the Documents tab. Scans, editable drafts and completed outputs stay inside SignLeaf on this iPhone. SignLeaf has no cloud sync. Use Share or Save to Files after saving to make another copy.

I cancelled the share sheet

Your completed file stays saved inside SignLeaf. Reopen the document and share the saved output again. The share sheet cannot confirm that another person received it.

Why can't I export a scan?

Add at least one visible handwritten signature or initial to the document before saving an output. SignLeaf exports signed scanned documents. A scan without a signature and a standalone signature image cannot be exported.

Purchases

How do I restore my subscription?

Open SignLeaf Profile and tap Restore Purchases while connected to the internet. Use the Apple Account that purchased the subscription. A pending approval may need to complete before access appears.

How do I cancel or change a subscription?

In SignLeaf Profile, tap Manage Subscription. You can also open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app or its documents does not cancel billing. See Apple's cancellation instructions.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store refund requests. Visit Apple's Report a Problem page using the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Privacy and deletion

You can remove documents and signatures in their libraries. Copies already shared to other apps or Files must be deleted there separately. A deleted library signature remains visible in documents where it was already placed.
