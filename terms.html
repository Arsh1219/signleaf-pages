What SignLeaf does

SignLeaf lets you scan paper documents or import photos, images and PDF files, capture your signature or initials with the camera, a photo, an image file or by drawing with your finger, clean up and reuse that handwriting, and add text, dates and checkmarks. You can save a signed document as a PDF containing all pages, or as one PNG or JPG image per page.

An output requires at least one visible handwritten signature or initial on the document. The current app does not export unsigned documents or a standalone signature image. Scanning, signature storage, editing and previewing are available without a subscription, subject to your device's available storage and capabilities.

You may use only content and signatures you own or have permission to use. Do not impersonate someone, forge a signature or authorization, or violate another person's rights. You retain your rights in your documents and signatures; we do not claim ownership of them.

Signatures you use

Capture, draw, import and place only your own signature or initials, or a signature that its owner has clearly authorized you to use for that document. Do not photograph, scan or import another person's signature to sign as them without their permission. Using a signature without authority can be forgery or fraud and may be unlawful.

By saving a signature in SignLeaf, you confirm that it is yours or that you have permission to use it. You are responsible for every signature you place and for having the authority to sign each document. SignLeaf cannot check who a signature belongs to.

Signing and checking your work

SignLeaf places an image of handwriting on a document. It does not verify a signer's identity or authority, provide notarization or a digital-signature certificate, or create a tamper-proof audit trail. Acceptance and legal effect depend on the document, recipient and applicable law. Check that this signing method is suitable for your intended use.

Review every page for legibility, missing content and correct placement before sending it. Lighting, paper quality, source images and device storage can affect results. A completed share sheet does not prove that another person received or accepted a document.
