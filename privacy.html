Privacy policy

Effective date: 9 September 2026

This policy explains how SignLeaf handles information when you use the app or visit our website. For privacy questions or requests, contact the SignLeaf developer at arshpreet@billionweb.co.

Your documents stay on your iPhone

SignLeaf scans paper documents and removes paper backgrounds from handwritten signatures on your device. Original scans, signature photos, cleaned signatures, text you add, drafts and completed files are stored locally. SignLeaf does not upload this content to our servers, Firebase or RevenueCat. There are no SignLeaf accounts or cloud document sync.

The app asks for camera access when you scan. Its system photo picker gives SignLeaf the images you choose. You can change camera permission in iPhone Settings. When you choose Share or Save to Files, iOS passes your selected output to the app or storage provider you choose; that provider's privacy terms apply.

Usage measurement and crash reporting

Usage and crash diagnostics are enabled by default to improve SignLeaf. We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand app use and compare subscription offers. Events contain categories and measurements such as scan success, page count, processing duration, export format, button actions and purchase outcomes. Documents, signature images, entered text and filenames are excluded. A randomly generated installation identifier links this activity to the subscription experiment; it does not contain your name or email. Apple and RevenueCat continue to process purchases. There is no in-app usage and diagnostics switch.

Firebase Crashlytics receives crash traces, app and operating system versions, device details and its own installation identifiers to help us fix failures. SignLeaf's custom error reports contain fixed error categories. We do not add your document contents, filenames, file paths or raw error messages to these reports. Firebase's infrastructure processes technical connection information when providing its services. See Firebase privacy information.

Subscriptions and attribution

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription records, product identifiers, subscription status, the installation identifier, subscription-offer assignment, storefront and trial-eligibility category to provide access, restore purchases and report subscription performance. We do not receive your payment-card details through SignLeaf.

RevenueCat also receives Apple's AdServices attribution token and, when available, campaign, ad group and keyword attribution returned by Apple. We use this to measure Apple Ads acquisition. SignLeaf does not collect IDFA, request tracking permission, or show third-party ads. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

You can delete documents and signatures in their libraries. Deleting a saved library signature does not remove copies already placed in documents. Deleting content leaves a small local save-history ledger and the installation's subscription-offer assignment; it does not cancel a subscription or delete Apple's purchase records. Files you shared elsewhere must be deleted separately.

Documents are not backed up or synced by SignLeaf. Keep copies of important completed files in a location you control. Removing the app or erasing the device can remove local work; SignLeaf cannot recover it from a server.

Analytics and diagnostic information is retained under the settings and retention rules of the services that process it. We use retention criteria based on the information needed to understand usage, investigate failures and maintain the app. Subscription records may need to remain available to restore access, resolve billing disputes and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Support correspondence is kept for handling your request and related follow-up. Retention may be longer where required by law or necessary to establish or defend legal claims. Contact us to ask about retention or request deletion of information associated with your use of SignLeaf.

Support messages and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, and use it to respond to your request. Please do not send confidential documents or signature images.

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising pixels or cookies to this website. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request details to serve and secure the site, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your choices and requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data. Contact arshpreet@billionweb.co. We may need information to locate the relevant installation or purchase record and verify a request. Do not send sensitive documents or signature images. We cannot identify you from the contents of a document because those contents stay on your device.

Processing and international services

We use information to provide app and subscription functionality, answer support requests, maintain reliability and measure usage. Depending on the information and applicable law, processing may be necessary to provide a requested service, meet a legal obligation, support legitimate interests in operating and improving the app, or rely on consent where required. You may object to processing or withdraw consent where applicable by contacting us.

Apple, Google Firebase, RevenueCat and our website and email providers may process information in countries outside your own. Their privacy information describes their processing locations and international-transfer arrangements. Contact us for questions about processing relevant to your use of SignLeaf.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or our practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
